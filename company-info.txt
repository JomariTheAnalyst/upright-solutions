Upright Solutions and Systems Consultancy Corp. (formerly Upright Systems Inc.) is a Philippine-based IT system integration, professional services, and software development company.

Originally established in 2015 as Upright Maritime Learning and Review Center Corp., the company first provided online learning solutions for students preparing for professional licensure examinations. Within five years, Upright evolved from e-learning services into developing integrated information systems for its sister companies—eventually expanding to serve multiple industries impressed by its technology solutions.

Today, Upright delivers a full suite of IT services, combining technical depth with a strong understanding of real-world business needs. Its core offerings include:

IT System Integration & Consultancy

Software Design & Development

Professional Services

Hardware Deployment & Maintenance

Upright Solutions supports a wide range of industries, including:

Government

Financial Services

Telecommunications

Energy & Utilities

Mining

Maritime

Health Care

Manufacturing

Hospitality

Logistics

Education

What sets Upright apart is its commitment to staying ahead of client needs—deeply understanding each client’s business, and delivering optimized IT solutions from requirements scoping to deployment, maintenance, and continuous enhancement. Upright always looks from the client’s perspective, and equally from the client’s customer’s perspective, to ensure every solution truly addresses real operational demands.
